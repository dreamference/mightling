Shigar

$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/dreamference/mightling/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
$ ling-admin server start      # yana sauke samfurin sau ɗaya (~20 GB), sannan ya fara bayar da shi

Yana aiki a kan NVIDIA DGX Spark da kowace na'urar GB10. Script ɗin yana tabbatar da kowane saukewa da checksums na fitowar, kuma yana nuna kowane umarnin sudo kafin ya gudanar da shi. Karanta shi tukuna idan kana so.

Clients na Mac da Linux

Sauran kwamfutocinka suna amfani da samfurin GB10 ta hanyar cibiyar sadarwa ta gida. A kan Mac ko wata na'urar Linux, umarnin da ke sama iri ɗaya yana shigar da client ɗin ling; yana gano GB10 da kansa.

Daga 1.7.2 yana kuma sanya manhajar desktop a cikin Applications a kan Mac, a shirye don buɗewa (--no-app yana ketare ta).

Client na Windows samfoti

> irm https://github.com/dreamference/mightling/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex

A cikin PowerShell. Yana shigarwa ba tare da haƙƙin mai gudanarwa ba (daga 1.7.2 har da manhajar desktop), sannan Windows ta tambaya sau ɗaya don kafa sandbox ɗin wakilin. Ba a sa hannu a kai ba tukuna, don haka dole a kashe Smart App Control. Karanta script ɗin.

Sauran hanyoyin shigarwa

Shafin fitowa yana kuma ɗauke da dmg na Mac (Mightling-<version>-arm64-preview.dmg, ko x64 don Intel) da na'urar shigarwa ta Windows (Mightling-Setup-<version>-x64.exe, ko arm64). Samfoti ne marasa sa hannu: idan an sauke su da browser, ana hana su a karon farko. Duba kowanne da SHA256SUMS mai sa hannu na fitowar tukuna.

  • macOS 15 da sama: ja Mightling zuwa Applications ka buɗe shi sau ɗaya, sannan System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. A macOS 14 da ƙasa, Control-danna manhajar › Open.
  • Windows: SmartScreen › More info › Run anyway. Dole a kashe Smart App Control.

Gwada waɗannan tukuna

Ko kuma ka jera aiki don daren yau da /night add <task>: Mightling yana aiki a kan jerin yayin da kake barci, kowane aiki a git branch ɗinsa, kai kuma kana duba branches ɗin da kofi a hannu.

$ ling "explain this repository to me like I'm new on the team"
# reads the code index (ling-code outline, def, refs, callers) instead of grepping,
# then walks you through the layout, the entry points and how they connect
$ ling "the tests are failing: find out why and fix them"
# runs the tests, reads the failure, edits the code and runs the tests again,
# asking before anything risky
> /night add --best-of 3 add input validation to the signup form, with tests
# tonight: three attempts, each in its own git worktree; the one the chooser picks
# is committed on its own branch, and nothing is merged or pushed
$ ling-admin night status      # the timer, the window and the queue
> /night apply <id>            # in the morning: bring the branch back as uncommitted changes

Lambarka ba ta barin teburinka — kuma za ka iya tabbatarwa

Wakilan rubuta lamba na cloud suna aika lambar tushenka zuwa sabar wasu. Mightling ba ya aika ta ko'ina: samfurin yana aiki a GB10 ɗinka, kuma babu abin da ke cikin Mightling da ke tuntuɓar waje. Babu nazarin amfani, babu metrics, babu shiga da asusu, babu rahotannin tsayawar manhaja (crash reports) na "ɓoyayyen suna". Wannan yana sa a iya amfani da shi ga lambar da ba a yarda ka ɗora ba: aikin abokin ciniki, bayanan da doka ke sarrafawa, kayayyakin da ba a fitar ba.

Umarni ɗaya yana gudanar da zaman Mightling na gaske a ƙarƙashin strace yana jera kowane haɗin cibiyar sadarwa da ya yi:

$ ling-admin audit egress
✔ Egress audit: pass
Network destinations:
   127.0.0.1:8000     29x  model server
   127.0.0.1:8767      1x  Gmail search service
DNS queries: none
Networked git commands: none

Komai ya tsaya a 127.0.0.1. Ko neman DNS ɗaya babu.

Kana buƙatar tabbaci mai ƙarfi?

Rubuta /airgapped on kuma kowane umarnin da wakili ke gudanarwa yana samun network namespace maras komai daga kernel na Linux. Ba roƙo ne na ladabi ga samfurin ba: kawai babu cibiyar sadarwa da za a kai. Full Access, wanda zai tsallake sandbox, ana ƙi shi a lokaci guda.

Me zai iya fita, kuma yaushe

Abin da kai ko wakili kuka nema kawai: binciken gidan yanar gizo (ta hanyar SearXNG na sirri a na'urarka), ɗaukar shafi, buƙatar karantawa kawai ga asusun Google da ka haɗa, ko duba sabuntawa. A /airgapped on, babu ko ɗaya daga cikinsu.

Ka karanta shi a matsayin kwatancen abokan tafiya, ba jerin matsayi ba. A kan ayyuka 100 iri ɗaya Mightling ya warware adadin da Qwen3-Coder-480B da GLM-4.6 suka warware, samfuran da suka fi shi girma sau da yawa, amma kowane layi yana amfani da wakili daban, kayan aiki daban kuma, ga layukan leaderboard, hotunan gwaji na x86 na hukuma, yayin da namu ya yi aiki a kan hotunan arm64 na al'umma. A yanayin refine zaman farko yana nazarin aikin ba tare da canza komai ba, sabon zama kuma ya yi shi: ya warware aiki ɗaya fiye da yanayin da aka saba (69 da 68, kowane ɓangare yana cin ayyukan da ɗayan ya rasa), da kusan sau 2.2 na lokaci a kowane aiki, don haka ya kasance na zaɓi (ling --refine).

Yadda ake kimanta ≈69%

Ayyuka 100 kashi ɗaya cikin biyar ne na Verified, don haka adadin dukan jerin kimantawa ne. An ɗora samfurin item-response (Rasch) mai parameter ɗaya a kan sakamakon kowane aiki na dukan gabatarwar Verified 175 na jama'a: kowane aiki yana da wahalarsa kuma kowane tsari yana da iyawarsa. Iyawar Mightling ita ce wadda ke bayyana ayyuka 68 da aka warware a kan waɗannan 100; ≈69% shi ne makin da ake tsammani na wannan iyawa a kan dukan 500 (regression na yau da kullum a kan makin da aka wallafa ya yarda cikin maki ɗaya). Don gwada shi, an ba kowace gabatarwar jama'a maki a kan waɗannan ayyuka 100 kaɗai, aka hasashen makinta na gaba ɗaya haka nan: kuskuren ya kasance maki 2.4 (RMSE), kuma kewayon da ke sama ±1.96 ne na wannan. Kowane lamba a teburin scripts/swe_bench_compare.py yana sake samar da ita daga bayanan kowane aiki na jama'a na SWE-bench/experiments.

An rarraba ayyuka 100 a repositories na Verified daidai gwargwado da dukan jerin, a cikin ayyukan da muhallin gwajinsu ke aiki kuma ya tabbata a arm64 (gold patch yana wucewa, canji maras komai yana faɗuwa). Tsarukan da aka wallafa suna samun maki 1.0 zuwa 1.5 ƙasa a kan waɗannan 100 fiye da a kan dukan 500, don haka samfurin ba mai sauƙi ba ne. Shin ina buƙatar GB10?

Eh, don gudanar da samfurin. Sauran kwamfutocinka suna amfani da shi a matsayin clients: Linux (Arm ko x86) da macOS da umarnin shigarwa iri ɗaya, Windows (Arm ko x86, samfoti) da layin PowerShell.

Eh: an canza sunan Puffin zuwa Mightling a 1.5. Gudanar da puffin update sau ɗaya, sannan puffin na ƙarshe. Yana mayar da zamanka, saitunanka da hanyoyin haɗinka, kuma daga nan umarnin shi ne ling.

Da gaske kyauta ne?

Eh. Mightling buɗaɗɗen tushe ne a ƙarƙashin AGPL, kuma weights ɗin samfurin kyauta ne. Abin da kake kashewa kawai shi ne wutar lantarki.

Me nake buƙata baya ga na'urar?

Tsarin aiki da take zuwa da shi (DGX OS 7, ko Ubuntu 24.04 inda mai samarwa ke bayarwa), Docker tare da NVIDIA Container Toolkit, Python 3 da Git. A Ubuntu na yau da kullum, ling-admin host check yana gaya maka abin da babu.

Shin wani abu yana kai intanet?

Abin da kai ko wakili kuka nema kawai, kamar binciken yanar gizo ta SearXNG a na'urarka, da saukewa na farko. /airgapped on yana kashe duka.

Wane samfuri yake gudanarwa?

Qwen3.8-27B a tsarin NVFP4 na 4-bit na NVIDIA, SGLang ke bayarwa tare da drafter na DFlash2, an zaɓa kuma an daidaita shi don GB10. Weights kusan 20 GB; kusan 38 GB na memory ɗin na'urar yana ragewa a fili yayin da yake aiki.

Ka riƙe lambarka a kan teburinka

Idan ka yi imanin lamba ya kamata ta zauna a teburin da aka rubuta ta, ka yi wa repository tauraro: haka sauran masu GB10 ke gano Mightling.

$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/dreamference/mightling/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
