Haka kuma a 1.7.0: Night Shift na iya gwada aiki ɗaya sau da yawa ya riƙe mafi kyawun gwaji (/night add --best-of N). Na'ura ɗaya na iya gudanar da gadoji na Signal da yawa. Kuma kwamfutar da ke ganin GB10 biyu a hanyar sadarwa ɗaya na iya zaɓar tsakaninsu ba tare da manta wanda ta riga ta sani ba. Bayanan fitowa da saukarwa.

Shigar

$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/dreamference/mightling/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
$ ling-admin server start      # yana sauke samfurin sau ɗaya (~20 GB), sannan ya fara shi
$ cd ~/your-project && ling    # shi ke nan

Wannan yana aiki a kan NVIDIA DGX Spark da kuma kowace na'urar GB10 ta daban. Rubutun yana duba kowane saukarwa da checksums na fitowar, kuma yana nuna maka kowane umarnin sudo kafin ya gudanar da shi. Za ka iya karanta rubutun tukuna.

Clients na Mac da Linux

Sauran kwamfutocinka suna aron samfurin GB10 ta hanyar sadarwar gidanka ko ofishinka. A Mac ko wata na'urar Linux, umarni ɗaya yana sanya ling, wato ɓangaren da kake rubutu a ciki. Yana samun GB10 da kansa.

Daga 1.7.2 yana kuma sanya manhajar tebur a Applications a kan Mac, a shirye don buɗewa. Ƙara --no-app idan ba ka so.

Client na Windows samfoti

> irm https://github.com/dreamference/mightling/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex

Rubuta wannan a PowerShell. Ba ya buƙatar haƙƙin mai gudanarwa (daga 1.7.2 haka ma manhajar tebur). Sai Windows ya tambaye ka sau ɗaya ka kafa wuri mai bango inda mai taimakon yake gudanar da umarni. Ba a sa wa mai shigarwar hannu tukuna ba, don haka dole a kashe Smart App Control. Karanta rubutun.

Sauran hanyoyin shigarwa

Shafin fitowar yana kuma da hoton diski na Mac (Mightling-<version>-arm64-preview.dmg, ko x64 don Intel) da mai shigar da Windows (Mightling-Setup-<version>-x64.exe, ko arm64). Samfuran gwaji ne marasa sa hannu, don haka idan ka sauke su a burauza, tsarinka yana tsayar da su a karon farko. Kafin ka buɗe ɗaya, duba shi da fayil ɗin SHA256SUMS mai sa hannu na fitowar.

  • macOS 15 da sabo: ja Mightling zuwa Applications ka buɗe shi sau ɗaya. Sai ka je System Settings › Privacy & Security ka danna Open Anyway. A macOS 14 da tsoho, danna manhajar tare da Control ka zaɓi Open.
  • Windows: SmartScreen › More info › Run anyway. Dole a kashe Smart App Control.

Gwada waɗannan tukuna

Ko ka bar aiki na yau da dare da /night add <task>. Mightling yana bi ta jerin yayin da kake barci, kowane aiki a reshen git nasa, kuma da safe ka duba rassan.

$ ling "explain this repository to me like I'm new on the team"
# reads the code index (ling-code outline, def, refs, callers) instead of grepping,
# then walks you through the layout, the entry points and how they connect
$ ling "the tests are failing: find out why and fix them"
# runs the tests, reads the failure, edits the code and runs the tests again,
# asking before anything risky
> /night add --best-of 3 add input validation to the signup form, with tests
# tonight: three attempts, each in its own git worktree; the one the chooser picks
# is committed on its own branch, and nothing is merged or pushed
$ ling-admin night status      # the timer, the window and the queue
> /night apply <id>            # in the morning: bring the branch back as uncommitted changes

🧭 Fihirisar lamba

Jerin inda aka ayyana abubuwa da wanda ke amfani da su a ko'ina cikin aikinka, don mai taimakon ya iya duba lambar kwamfuta ba sai ya nemo ta ta hasashe ba.

Bincike yana wucewa ta SearXNG na sirri a na'urarka, don haka babu asusun bincike kuma babu maɓallin da za a nema. Tambayoyin suna dai fita zuwa yanar gizo don a amsa su.

🌙 Night Shift

Rubuta /night add <task> kafin barci. Da safe kowane aiki ya kammala a reshen git nasa. Ba a haɗa ko a tura komai sai ka yi da kanka.

Abin da za ka samu

Manhajar tebur ta 1.7.0 tana haɗa hanyoyin amfani da Mightling biyu a taga ɗaya: Ask, inda kake yin tambayoyi, da Work, inda mai taimakon yake gyara lambar kwamfutarka. Work yana da akwati na rubuta ayyuka (za ka iya haɗa fayiloli, skills da umarni), da faifai don duba canje-canje kafin ka riƙe ko ka soke su, da kwafin aiki daban-daban na lambar kwamfutarka (worktrees), da shafukan tasha, da itacen fayiloli kusa da tattaunawar. A sauran kwamfutocinka, manhajar tana magana da GB10 naka, wanda take samu a hanyar sadarwarka.

Lambar kwamfutarka tana kan teburinka, kuma za ka iya tabbatar da haka

Mai taimakon rubuta lambar kwamfuta na cloud yana aika lambar kwamfutarka zuwa kwamfutocin da na wani ne. Mightling ba ya aika ta ko'ina. Samfurin yana aiki a GB10 naka, kuma babu komai a Mightling da ke tuntuɓar waje: babu ƙidayar amfani, babu auna, babu shiga, ko rahotannin rushewa na "ɓoyayye". Don haka za ka iya amfani da shi a kan lambar da ba a yarda ka ɗora ba, kamar aikin abokin ciniki, ko bayanai masu kariya, ko samfurin da ba a fitar ba tukuna.

Umarni ɗaya yana gudanar da zaman Mightling na gaske a ƙarƙashin strace, kayan aikin da ke kallon kowace kira da shiri ke yi, sannan ya jera kowane haɗin hanyar sadarwa da zaman ya buɗe:

$ ling-admin audit egress

Network destinations:
   127.0.0.1:8000     29x  model server
   127.0.0.1:8767      1x  Gmail search service
DNS queries: none
Networked git commands: none

Duka sun tsaya a 127.0.0.1, wato na'urar tana magana da kanta. Ba a yi ko binciken DNS ɗaya ba (binciken DNS shi ne tambayar intanet adireshin wani suna).

Kana buƙatar tabbaci mai ƙarfi?

Rubuta /airgapped on. Kowane umarni da mai taimakon ya gudanar zai sami hanyar sadarwa maras komai daga kernel na Linux. Ba roƙo mai ladabi ga samfurin ba ne. Kawai babu hanyar sadarwa da za a kai. A lokaci guda, Full Access, wanda zai kewaye sandbox, ana ƙi shi.

Me zai iya fita, kuma yaushe

Abin da kai ko mai taimakon ya nema kawai: binciken yanar gizo (ta hanyar SearXNG na sirri da ke aiki a na'urarka, ko da yake binciken kansa yana fita zuwa yanar gizo), ɗauko shafin yanar gizo, buƙatar karatu kawai zuwa asusun Google da ka haɗa, ko duba sabuntawa. Da /airgapped on, babu ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan da ke faruwa.

SWE-bench Verified, an auna a kan DGX Spark guda

SWE-bench Verified tarin matsaloli 500 ne na gaske da aka ɗauko daga shirye-shiryen buɗaɗɗen tushe. A kowace ɗaya, dole mai taimakon ya canza lambar kwamfuta har gwaje-gwajen su ci. A 2026-10-08 mun ba Mightling 1.5.1 guda 100 daga cikinsu, da samfurin Qwen3.8-27B, a kan DGX Spark guda ba tare da intanet ba. Ya yi ƙoƙari ɗaya a kowace, a yanayinsa na asali, kuma ya warware 68 cikin 100. Idan muka faɗaɗa wannan zuwa 500 duka, kimarmu kusan 69% ce, da ƙarin ko ragi na maki kaɗan (kewayon 95% daga 63.9 zuwa 73.4% ne). Mun auna wannan da kanmu, a na'urarmu, a kan samfurin matsalolin. Ba shigarwa ba ce a hukumar jerin sunayen shugabanni.

Karanta wannan tebur a matsayin hanyar ganin wanda ke kusa, ba a matsayin jeri ba. A kan matsaloli 100 iri ɗaya, Mightling ya warware daidai da Qwen3-Coder-480B da GLM-4.6, samfura da suka fi shi girma sau da yawa. Amma kowane layi yana amfani da wani shirin mai taimako da wata na'ura daban, kuma layukan jerin shugabanni sun yi amfani da hotunan gwaji na hukuma don kwamfutocin x86, yayin da namu ya yi aiki a kan hotunan al'umma don arm64. Akwai kuma wani yanayi na biyu, mai suna refine. Zama na farko yana nazarin matsalar bai canza komai ba, sannan sabon zama ya yi aikin. Ya warware matsala ɗaya fiye da na asali (69 da 68, kuma kowane yanayi ya warware wasu da ɗayan ya rasa), kuma ya ɗauki kusan sau 2.2 na lokaci a kowace matsala. Don haka a kashe yake sai ka nema da ling --refine.

Yadda muke kiyasin 69%

Matsaloli 100 kashi ɗaya cikin biyar ne na Verified, don haka lambar 500 duka kiyasi ce. Ga yadda muka yi ta. Ɗauki sakamakon dukan abubuwan da aka miƙa 175 na Verified a fili, matsala bayan matsala. Ba kowace matsala wahala, ba kowane tsari iyawa, da samfurin item-response (Rasch) mai sigogi ɗaya. Iyawar Mightling ita ce wadda za ta warware 68 a cikin waɗannan matsaloli 100, kuma kusan 69% shi ne abin da wannan iyawar za ta samu a 500 duka. Regression mai sauƙi a kan makin da aka buga yana yarda a cikin maki ɗaya. Mun kuma gwada hanyar a kan wasu: ga kowane abin da aka miƙa a fili, mun yi amfani da makinsa a kan waɗannan matsaloli 100 kaɗai don hasashen makinsa a 500 duka. Kuskuren ya kasance maki 2.4 (RMSE), kuma kewayon da ke sama sau 1.96 ne na wannan a kowane gefe. Za a iya sake samun kowace lamba a cikin tebur ta scripts/swe_bench_compare.py, daga bayanan kowace matsala na jama'a a SWE-bench/experiments.

Mun raba matsaloli 100 a tsakanin shirye-shiryen da ke cikin Verified da adadi ɗaya da na cikakken tarin. Mun zaɓe su daga matsalolin da shirin gwajinsu ke aiki kuma ya tabbata a arm64 (gyaran da ya dace yana cin gwajin, rashin yin komai kuma yana faɗuwa). Tsare-tsaren da aka buga suna samun maki 1.0 zuwa 1.5 ƙasa a kan waɗannan 100 fiye da 500 duka, don haka samfurin ba mai sauƙi ba ne. Duk da haka ba shigarwa ba ce a jerin shugabanni, domin ƙungiyar SWE-bench ce ke tantance su a na'urorinsu.

Tambayoyi

Shin ina buƙatar GB10?

Eh, don gudanar da samfurin. Sauran kwamfutocinka abokan ciniki ne da ke aronsa: Linux (Arm ko x86) da macOS da umarnin shigarwa iri ɗaya, da Windows (Arm ko x86, samfurin gwaji) da layin PowerShell.

Na shigar da Puffin. Wannan shiri ɗaya ne?

Eh. An canza sunan Puffin zuwa Mightling a 1.5. Gudanar da puffin update sau ɗaya, sannan ka gudanar da puffin sau na ƙarshe. Yana kai zamanka, saitunanka da hanyoyin haɗinka, kuma bayan haka umarnin shi ne ling.

Da gaske ba ya kashe kuɗi?

Babu kuɗi don shirin, babu kuma don samfurin. Mightling buɗaɗɗen tushe ne ƙarƙashin AGPL, kuma nauyin samfurin kyauta ne a yi amfani da shi. Kana biyan na'urar da wutar lantarkin da take ci.

Me nake buƙata baya ga na'urar?

Tsarin aiki da ke zuwa tare da ita (DGX OS 7, ko Ubuntu 24.04 a inda NVIDIA ke bayarwa), Docker da NVIDIA Container Toolkit, Python 3 da Git. A Ubuntu na yau da kullum, ling-admin host check yana gaya maka abin da ya rasa.

Shin wani abu yana fita zuwa intanet ko da yaushe?

Abin da kai ko mai taimakon ya nema kawai, kamar binciken yanar gizo (ta SearXNG da ke aiki a na'urarka) da saukarwa a karon farko. Da /airgapped on, duka an kashe su.

Wane samfuri yake gudanarwa?

Qwen3.8-27B, a tsarin 4-bit na NVIDIA mai suna NVFP4, SGLang ke bayarwa tare da ƙaramin mataimaki mai hasashe mai suna DFlash2 drafter. Mun zaɓa mun kuma gyara duka don GB10. Nauyin yana ɗaukar kusan 20 GB, kuma yayin da yake aiki, kusan 38 GB na ƙwaƙwalwar na'urar yana nan a wofi.

Ka riƙe lambarka a kan teburinka

Idan kana ganin lambar kwamfuta ta zauna a kan teburin da aka rubuta ta, ba wa ma'ajiyar tauraro a GitHub. Haka sauran masu GB10 ke samun Mightling.
